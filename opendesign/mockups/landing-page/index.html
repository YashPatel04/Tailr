Tailr is fully open-source and self-hosted. Your data stays on your infrastructure. No vendor lock-in, no usage caps, no surprises.

⬡
Self-hosted
Docker Compose up and running in minutes. PostgreSQL, Redis, and texlive — all containerized. No external services required.
λ
LaTeX-native
Full LaTeX parsing and compilation. Your formatting is preserved exactly — no conversions, no approximations, no broken layouts.
◈
Bring your own LLM
Connect any OpenAI-compatible provider. Use GPT-4, Claude, Llama, or a local model. Your API key, your choice.
