Tailr — Redline Logo System

Refined, detailed, and finished. Six variations on the document-cut theme.

01 — Mark Variations
The Document Cut
Each variation starts from the same idea: a resume page with a diagonal cut. The cut is the action — tailoring, editing, proofreading. The edge color is the brand accent.
Classic Redline
Document page with a single clean diagonal cut. Colored edge reveals the tailoring action. Most minimal of the set.
Scissors Cut
Scissors mid-cut through the document. Blades follow the diagonal line. Handles in brand blue. Action frozen in motion.
Diagonal Stitch
Thread sewn diagonally across the page. Stitch holes, needle at one end, thread tail at the other. Craft meets precision.
Layered Cut
Three stacked pages, each slightly offset. The cut goes through all layers, revealing colored edges at three depths. Rich and dimensional.
Scissors Action
Scissors in full open position, blades crossing the page diagonally. Handles frame the document. Most dynamic of the set.
Thread & Needle
Full sewing scene: needle with thread knot, curved stitch segments across the page, spool tail at top. Most narrative and tactile.
